# Dhun — policy pages

The privacy policy and terms for the **Dhun** Android app, served by GitHub Pages.

- Privacy Policy — `privacy.html`
- Terms of Use — `terms.html`

## Why this repository exists separately

Google Play will not list an app without a live, public privacy policy URL. The app's own
repository is private, and GitHub Pages only serves private repositories on a paid plan —
so the pages live here, in a public repository of their own, and nothing else does.

## Do not rewrite history here

These are legal documents. The commit history is the record of what the policy said on
any given date, which is the answer if someone ever disputes what they agreed to.

Never force-push. Never amend a published commit. Correcting a mistake means a new
commit, the same as correcting anything else.
