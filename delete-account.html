Delete your account

Dhun

You can delete your Dhun account and everything in it — your profile and conversations — without the app installed. A permission you gave as a parent is kept so your child keeps access, until you withdraw it — see the Privacy Policy, section 7.

If you still have the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. This is the fastest way: it happens immediately, in the app, with no waiting period.

If you no longer have the app

Email support.dhun.pragya@gmail.com from the email address your account signed in with, and ask for your account to be deleted. Tell us that email address if you are writing from a different one. We will confirm once it is done.

This cannot be undone. Deletion is immediate and permanent — see the Privacy Policy, section 7.
