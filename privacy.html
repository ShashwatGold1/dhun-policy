5. Children

Dhun is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them: an account whose date of birth is under 13 is refused before that date is stored. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

For everyone under 18 — which the DPDP Act counts as a child, and which is stricter than the age GDPR sets in any European country — these rules apply, wherever you live:

  • An account belonging to someone under 18 cannot ask a question until a parent or guardian has linked to it and allowed it.
  • The link is made by the young person generating a short code and the adult entering it. Both sides have to act. Nobody can attach themselves to a child's account.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time. The young person is blocked from their very next question onwards.
  • A guardian can never read a young person's conversations. They can see that the account is being used. Not the words. This is deliberate: someone who believes they are being read stops asking the questions they are embarrassed by, and those are often the questions that matter most.
  • We do not show children advertising targeted using their personal data, and we do not track children across other apps or websites.

Age is self-declared. We cannot verify it, and we do not ask for documents to prove it — collecting identity documents from children would create a far greater risk than the one it solves.

6. Advertising

On the free plan you may choose to watch a short video to earn more questions. These are served by Google AdMob. Watching is always a choice — nothing is shown unless you ask for it.

Ads shown to anyone under 18, or whose age we do not know, are non-personalised: they are not selected using anything about you. If you are an adult in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Google asks for your consent before any ad is personalised, and you can say no. Google may still process a device identifier to show and count the ad. Google's handling of that is covered by Google's privacy policy.

On the Yearly and Pay-as-you-go plans there are no ads at all.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may:

  • See what we hold about you.
  • Correct anything wrong. Most of it you can edit yourself in Settings.
  • Delete your account and everything in it.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if we have not resolved it.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to receive your data in a portable form, to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We use your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), on your consent where we ask for it (personalised ads), and to keep the service secure and working (legitimate interests).

If you are under 18, a linked parent or guardian may exercise these on your behalf.

Email support.dhun.pragya@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Security

  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • We never hold your password — sign-in is Google's, and no password passes through our code at any point.
  • Every request is checked against your sign-in before any data is returned. Asking for someone else's conversation returns nothing, whoever you are.
  • Keys and credentials live only on the server, never in the app on your phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Act requires.

10. Where this applies

Dhun is offered worldwide. It is run from India, and Indian law (the DPDP Act) always applies. Where the law of your country gives you more protection — such as the GDPR in Europe — you have that protection too.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
