If you are under 18, a parent, guardian or teacher must allow your account before you can ask anything. Until they do, the app will not answer.

5. Children

Ai-Tutor is intended for students aged 11 and above, so most of the people using it are children under the DPDP Act, which counts anyone under 18 as a child.

  • An account belonging to someone under 18 cannot ask a question until a parent, guardian or teacher has linked to it and allowed it.
  • The link is made by the student generating a short code and the adult entering it. Both sides have to act. Nobody can attach themselves to a child's account.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time. The student is blocked from their very next question onwards.
  • A guardian can never read a student's conversations. They can see that the student is working and on what. Not the words. This is deliberate: a student who believes someone is reading along stops asking the questions they are embarrassed by, and those are the questions a tutor exists for.
  • We do not show children advertising targeted using their personal data, and we do not track children across other apps or websites.

Age is self-declared. We cannot verify it, and we do not ask for documents to prove it — collecting identity documents from children would create a far greater risk than the one it solves.

6. Advertising

On the free plan you may choose to watch a short video to earn more questions. These are served by Google AdMob. Watching is always a choice — nothing is shown unless you ask for it.

Ads shown to accounts we know to be children are non-personalised: they are not selected using anything about you. Google may still process a device identifier to show and count the ad. Google's handling of that is covered by Google's privacy policy.

On the Yearly and Pay-as-you-go plans there are no ads at all.

ConversationsUntil you delete them, or you delete your account.

The 30 days exist so a deletion made by mistake can be undone. After that it cannot be recovered by us or by you.

Records we are required to keep for tax or accounting — that a payment happened, and for how much — are kept as long as the law requires. They do not contain your questions.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • See what we hold about you.
  • Correct anything wrong. Most of it you can edit yourself in Settings.
  • Delete your account and everything in it.
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if we have not resolved it.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to.

If you are under 18, a linked parent or guardian may exercise these on your behalf.

Email support.aitutor.atpragya@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Security

  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • We never hold your password — sign-in is Google's, and no password passes through our code at any point.
  • Every request is checked against your sign-in before any data is returned. Asking for someone else's conversation returns nothing, whoever you are.
  • Keys and credentials live only on the server, never in the app on your phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Act requires.

10. Where this applies
